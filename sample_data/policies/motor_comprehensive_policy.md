# Meridian Motor Comprehensive Policy — Policy Wording v3.2

## Section 1 — Policy Period and Eligibility
1.1 Cover applies only to incidents occurring within the policy period stated on the schedule. Policies are issued for 12 months from the inception date.
1.2 The insured vehicle must be the vehicle described in the schedule and driven by the policyholder or a named driver holding a valid licence.
1.3 Policy numbers for this product are in the format MC-XXXXXXX (e.g. MC-2041877).

## Section 2 — Covered Perils
2.1 Accidental collision damage to the insured vehicle, including collision with another vehicle, object, animal or road furniture.
2.2 Theft of the insured vehicle, or damage caused by attempted theft, provided the vehicle was locked and keys were not left inside.
2.3 Fire, lightning and explosion.
2.4 Storm, flood and hail damage.
2.5 Malicious damage and vandalism.
2.6 Windscreen and glass damage.

## Section 3 — Exclusions
3.1 Loss or damage occurring while the vehicle is driven by a person under the influence of alcohol above the legal limit or drugs.
3.2 Loss or damage occurring while the vehicle is used for hire, reward, racing, pace-making or speed testing.
3.3 Mechanical, electrical or electronic breakdown, wear and tear, depreciation, or gradual deterioration.
3.4 Loss of or damage to personal belongings inside the vehicle exceeding the Personal Effects sub-limit in Section 5.
3.5 Damage to tyres unless caused by a covered collision at the same time.
3.6 Loss occurring while the vehicle is driven by an unlicensed driver or a driver not named on the schedule where the schedule restricts drivers.
3.7 Any claim where the incident occurred before the inception date or after expiry of the policy period.

## Section 4 — Deductible (Excess)
4.1 A standard excess of 500 applies to each collision claim.
4.2 An additional young-driver excess of 300 applies where the driver at the time of loss is under 25 years of age.
4.3 A theft excess of 750 applies to each theft claim.
4.4 No excess applies to windscreen-only claims up to the glass sub-limit.

## Section 5 — Limits and Sub-limits
5.1 The maximum amount payable for damage to the insured vehicle is the market value of the vehicle at the time of loss.
5.2 Windscreen and glass: sub-limit 1,000 per claim.
5.3 Personal effects in the vehicle: sub-limit 300 per claim.
5.4 Towing and storage following a covered incident: sub-limit 400 per claim.
5.5 Hire car following a covered collision: up to 30 per day for a maximum of 14 days.

## Section 6 — Claim Conditions
6.1 The policyholder must notify the insurer of any incident within 30 days of the incident date. Late notification may result in the claim being declined unless the delay is reasonably explained.
6.2 Theft, vandalism and hit-and-run incidents must be reported to the police within 24 hours and the police report reference must be supplied with the claim.
6.3 The following documents are required for every collision claim: completed claim form, photographs of the damage, at least one repair estimate from a licensed repairer, and driver's licence of the driver at the time of loss.
6.4 The policyholder must not admit liability or make any offer of settlement to third parties without the insurer's written consent.
6.5 Repairs above 2,000 must not commence until the insurer has approved the repair estimate, except emergency repairs necessary to make the vehicle safe.
6.6 The insurer may appoint an assessor to inspect the vehicle before authorising repairs.

## Section 7 — Fraud
7.1 If any claim is fraudulent or exaggerated, or if any false declaration is made in support of a claim, all benefit under this policy is forfeited and the policy may be cancelled from the date of the fraudulent act.
