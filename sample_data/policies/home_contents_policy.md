# Meridian Home & Contents Policy — Policy Wording v2.0

## Section 1 — Policy Period and Insured Property
1.1 Cover applies to the residential address shown on the schedule for incidents within the policy period.
1.2 Policy numbers for this product are in the format HC-XXXXXXX.
1.3 "Contents" means household goods and personal belongings owned by the policyholder or family members permanently residing at the insured address.

## Section 2 — Covered Perils (Buildings and Contents)
2.1 Fire, smoke, lightning and explosion.
2.2 Storm, flood and water escaping from any fixed domestic water or heating installation, washing machine or dishwasher.
2.3 Theft or attempted theft, and burglary involving forcible and violent entry to the home.
2.4 Malicious damage and vandalism.
2.5 Impact by vehicle, aircraft, falling trees or aerials.
2.6 Accidental breakage of fixed glass, sanitary ware and ceramic hobs.

## Section 3 — Exclusions
3.1 Theft from the home not involving forcible and violent entry, unless the thief is not a member of the household and entry was gained by deception.
3.2 Theft of contents from the open, garden, balcony or any unlocked outbuilding.
3.3 Loss or damage caused by gradual deterioration, wet or dry rot, mould, wear and tear, or lack of maintenance.
3.4 Water damage caused by a failure to maintain seals, grouting or sealant, or by rising damp.
3.5 Damage occurring while the home has been unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days.
3.6 Loss of cash, bank notes or coins exceeding 500.
3.7 Loss or damage to money, credit cards or documents unless specified on the schedule.
3.8 Any loss caused by the deliberate act of the policyholder or a household member.

## Section 4 — Deductible (Excess)
4.1 A standard excess of 250 applies to each contents claim.
4.2 An escape-of-water excess of 500 applies to each claim under 2.2 for water escaping from installations.
4.3 A subsidence excess of 1,000 applies where subsidence cover is shown on the schedule.

## Section 5 — Limits and Sub-limits
5.1 Total contents sum insured is stated on the schedule.
5.2 Single-item limit: no single item of contents is covered above 2,500 unless individually specified on the schedule.
5.3 Valuables (jewellery, watches, furs, works of art, collectables): total sub-limit of 10,000 per claim and 2,500 per item unless specified.
5.4 Contents in the open or in outbuildings: sub-limit 1,000.
5.5 Food in freezers: sub-limit 500.
5.6 Alternative accommodation following an insured event rendering the home uninhabitable: up to 20% of the contents sum insured.

## Section 6 — Claim Conditions
6.1 Incidents must be notified to the insurer within 30 days of discovery.
6.2 Theft, burglary, vandalism and malicious damage must be reported to the police within 48 hours and a crime reference number supplied.
6.3 For each item claimed above 500, proof of ownership and value (receipt, valuation, bank statement or photograph) is required.
6.4 Escape-of-water claims require a plumber's report identifying the source of the leak.
6.5 The policyholder must take all reasonable steps to prevent further loss and to preserve damaged items for inspection.
6.6 Settlement is on a new-for-old basis for contents less than 5 years old; otherwise market value applies.

## Section 7 — Fraud
7.1 Fraudulent or exaggerated claims void all benefit under this policy.
